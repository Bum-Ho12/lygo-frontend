The problem

Law firms run on paper, inboxes and memory.

Hours lost to finding and re-typing

Documents arrive on paper, by email and on shared drives. Advocates search, read and re-type the same details, case after case.

Risks spotted late, or missed

A 14-day notice period, an exclusion, a cancellation clause. It's easy to catch it too late when it's buried on page nine.

Clients call to ask what it means

Clients don't understand their own policies, contracts and notices, so routine explanations and status updates eat up the firm's day.

Data protection with ad-hoc tools

Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 expects controls that laptops, USB sticks and personal email can't give.

The solution

One workspace per firm. Every document read, checked and explained.

1

Bring it in

Upload, drag in, or photograph paper. Scans are read with OCR. Files are virus-checked and de-duplicated.

2

Lygo reads it

It splits clauses, identifies the document type, and pulls out parties, amounts, dates and periods.

3

Rules check it

The firm's rule packs flag risky clauses and missing pieces, with a plain explanation of each.

4

People decide

Advocates confirm or dismiss findings and research with an assistant that cites its sources. Clients see what's been confirmed.

The assistant

Answers that show their sources, or say they don't know.

Cited, every time

Answers draw on the firm's documents, its knowledge base and Kenyan law. Each claim links to the passage it came from, and unverifiable citations are dropped.

Built for legal work

Research notes, case outlines, document summaries, fact-checking of citations, version comparison and a matter chronology.

Safe with clients

Trust

Built for privileged data from day one.

  • Firm isolation in the database, enforced row by row rather than by app code alone
  • Clients see only what's finished and confirmed, enforced by the API
  • Two-step sign-in for firm and platform staff, with an audit trail of every change
  • The firm chooses where files live: Lygo storage, or its own bucket, with residency recorded
  • Shaped around Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019: data processing agreements, minimal data, PII kept out of logs
  • Every firm is reviewed, with its LSK admission number checked, before its workspace opens
